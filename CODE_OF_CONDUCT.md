# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas, not people.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Keep project discussions relevant and professional.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized
content, and publishing private information without permission are not allowed.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through the
contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include
secrets, meeting audio, or personal data in a report.

Maintainers will review reports confidentially and may remove content or limit
participation when necessary to keep the community safe.
